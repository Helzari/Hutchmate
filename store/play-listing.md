## Full description

Hutchmate is the breeding companion for rabbit and guinea pig keepers. Plan pairings, predict colours, keep your herd and litter records in one place, and act quickly when an animal is unwell.

COLOUR & COAT CALCULATOR
Pick a doe and a buck, or a sow and a boar, and see every possible colour with its odds, plus coat type and size. Hutchmate warns you about risky pairings such as charlies, peanuts from two true dwarfs and lethal whites from two roan guinea pigs.

BREED FOR A COLOUR
Choose the colour you want. Hutchmate ranks every pairing in your herd by the chance of producing it and shows which new animal would be worth bringing in.

PEDIGREES
Add each animal's sire and dam and Hutchmate estimates the hidden genes it is likely to carry, for more accurate predictions.

SIMULATE A LITTER
Watch a random litter appear, drawn from the real odds.

IDENTIFY A COLOUR
Add photos and answer a few questions to get the most likely colours, each with a confidence score.

DUE DATES & LITTER LOG
Enter a mating date for the due date, the normal window and a timeline from mating to new homes. Record births and track every litter.

HEALTH CHECK
Emergency signs, a symptom checker by body area, what to do now, a health log for each animal and a summary to send to your vet.

BUILT FOR BREEDERS
• 19 rabbit breeds and 13 guinea pig breeds, each with breed notes
• Colour guide with genes and what the parents need to carry
• Works offline; your records stay on your device
• Light, dark, night and high-contrast modes, and larger text
• Built-in user guide

HUTCHMATE PRO
Everything above is free. Pro adds cloud backup so your records are safe and can be restored on any phone, with more features planned.

Hutchmate is a guide, not a guarantee. Genetics results are estimates, and the health checker is not veterinary advice. If you are worried about an animal, contact your vet.
