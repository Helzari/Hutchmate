Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Hutchmate is a breeding companion app for rabbits and guinea pigs, operated by Helzari in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and what we do with it.

The short version

  • Your animals, litters and health records are stored on your own device. We can't see them.
  • You only need an account for Hutchmate Pro. If you create one, we store your email address and, if you use cloud backup, a copy of your records.
  • No adverts, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Email addressIf you create an accountTo sign you in and identify your plan
PasswordIf you create an accountStored only as a salted, one-way hash; we never see your actual password
Plan and gift-code useIf you have an accountTo give you the features you're entitled to
Your herd, litter, health-log and vet-contact recordsOnly if you have Pro and use cloud backupTo restore your records on your devices
IP addressWhen the app contacts our serverUsed briefly to limit repeated sign-in attempts; not kept in a profile

Without an account, nothing you enter leaves your device.

Photos

In the Android app and at hutchmate.app, the "Identify" colour check analyses photos on your device. Photos are not uploaded or stored by us.

Where your data is kept

Account and backup data is stored with Cloudflare, Inc., which hosts our website and database and acts as our data processor. Data may be processed outside the UK under Cloudflare's standard safeguards.

Legal basis

We process account data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract) and limited technical data to keep the service secure (legitimate interests).

How long we keep it

We keep your account and backup until you delete your account. Sign-in attempt counters are short-lived.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings → Hutchmate Pro, sign in and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, your plan and your cloud backup from our servers straight away. Records stored on your own device are not affected; remove them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

If you can't sign in, email support@hutchmate.app from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to or restrict how we use it. Email support@hutchmate.app. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Hutchmate is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
